Required interfaces/content structure:
- hud.ts mountHud(root, store, send): top bar (week/day, whose turn, my gold/hp/level), side scoreboard (all players: name, class, gold, towns, score via computeScore), and a context panel switched on state.phase when it is my turn: roll -> Roll button; choose-path -> text 'Choose a highlighted tile' plus buttons for each option (also wires setPathHandler from src/client/render/board.ts to send choosePath); battle -> battle panel; shop -> shop panel; town -> town panel; end-turn -> End Turn button; game-over -> game-over panel for everyone. Not my turn: 'Waiting for <name>'
- battle.ts: shows both combatants (hp bars, monster name and drawn portrait via art.drawMonster if present else a coloured box), buttons Attack, Defend, Skill (class skill name), Item (consumables list), Flee; shows last round events
- shop.ts: lists shopStock(tileId) items with price and stat mods, Buy (disabled when unaffordable), Sell from own inventory at half price, Leave
- inventory.ts: lists inventory with Equip/Use buttons, shows equipped slots; available from the HUD when not in battle or choose-path
- town.ts: own town shows Upgrade (cost 80 x level, disabled at level 3 or unaffordable) and Leave; rival town shows Capture (150 x level) and Leave
- log.ts: last 50 entries of state.log plus event-derived messages
- gameover.ts: winner banner and final ranking by score
Edge/error cases:
- defeated player sees a respawn notice
- shop with nothing affordable
- battle where the defender is another player (both see the panel, only attacker acts)
